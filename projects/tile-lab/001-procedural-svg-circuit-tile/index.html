THE IDEA

Wrap the drawing. Keep the rhythm.

Every motif repeats at (x + i × 100, y + j × 100), for integer grid positions i and j. A phase shift adds the same X/Y offset to all copies. When a trace leaves one edge, its neighbor supplies the continuation at the opposite edge.

Try X = 100, Y = 0: it matches the starting pattern. Then try both at 50 and turn on the boundaries. The grid stays fixed while the artwork wraps through it.
