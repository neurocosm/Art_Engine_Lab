# Untrue Band Studio

**Status:** ON THE SHELF — Concept / Brainstorming  
**Parent:** Art Engine Lab  
**Development:** Not started  
**Working title:** Untrue Band Studio

## The Vision

An experimental creative design studio for inventing fictional musical artists, bands, concerts, venues, tours, and related merchandise.

The central idea is simple: **Bands that never existed. Shirts that look like they should have.**

Generate original, believable, funny, mysterious, nostalgic, or completely absurd fictional musical identities, along with professional-quality artwork suitable for printing.

## Creative Possibilities

- Fictional band names, logos, and musical identities
- Era-specific design from the 1950s through modern times and imagined futures
- Genres including rock, jazz, psychedelic, punk, metal, disco, progressive, electronic, alternative, experimental, and fictional genres
- Fake albums, concert tours, supporting acts, festivals, and tour dates
- Fictional venues, arenas, cities, and concert histories
- Vintage concert shirts, promotional posters, and memorabilia
- Authentic-looking aged or distressed print aesthetics
- Front and back T-shirt artwork

## The Design Studio

A possible workflow:

1. Choose an era, genre, and visual personality.
2. Brainstorm or generate fictional band names with AI assistance.
3. Develop an original logo and visual identity.
4. Select a historical or futuristic art direction.
5. Generate optional albums, tours, venues, and concert dates.
6. Arrange artwork using editable visual layers.
7. Experiment with color, typography, texture, alignment, and effects.
8. Preview the designs on apparel.
9. Export artwork suitable for printing.

The user should retain creative control and be able to edit or regenerate individual elements without rebuilding the entire composition.

## Potential Technologies

- **SVG:** Scalable logos, lettering, shapes, vector illustrations, and print compositions
- **Canvas 2D:** Texture processing, compositing, previews, and raster export
- **WebGL:** Optional GPU-driven effects, distortions, lighting, and visual experimentation
- **AI:** Creative brainstorming, naming, composition suggestions, and design interpretation

The rendering architecture has not been selected. Rasterized effects should not be described as true vectors.

## Print and Merchandise

Explore SVG and high-resolution PNG exports, transparent backgrounds, print sizing, color considerations, and shirt mockups.

Potential uses include T-shirts, sweatshirts, posters, stickers, and other merchandise.

## Originality Principles

The fictional identities should be original. Capture the visual culture of musical eras without duplicating recognizable existing band identities, trademarked names, logos, or protected artwork.

Before commercial use, names and artwork should be checked for potential conflicts.

## Possible First Experiment

Create a minimal browser-based concept generator that:

- Selects an era and genre
- Generates fictional band names
- Produces a basic SVG logo treatment
- Invents a venue and concert year
- Arranges the design into a printable shirt graphic
- Exports SVG artwork

No AI provider, framework, or specific rendering architecture is required at this stage.

## Shared AI Assistance — Historical Accuracy ↔ Creative License

**Idea on the shelf; not implemented.** Untrue Band Studio may use the Art Engine Lab-wide [Shared AI Assistance spec](../../docs/AI_ASSISTANCE.md) as a creative collaborator. The artist could ask for invented band identities, typography treatments, era-specific stage lore, tour locations, and T-shirt concepts, then inspect and edit every suggestion.

A signature **Creative Authenticity** slider (or vintage control-panel knob) would steer the direction:

- **Historically Accurate (0):** A fictional band with period-plausible music genre, concert-poster design, print techniques, venues, typography and language. "Accurate" describes its historical **style**, not the reality of the band.
- **Balanced (50):** Credible nostalgia mixed with original band mythology, playful copy and subtle musician/techie Easter eggs.
- **Creative License (100):** Invented musical genres, improbable reunion tours, impossible amplifiers, alternate-history venues and outlandishly funny shirts — all intentionally fictional.

**Example:** "Design an original 1977 progressive-rock tour shirt for a band that never existed. Include a made-up arena and a hidden joke that an electronics engineer would appreciate." Moving the control changes the plausibility of the inventions while preserving an original design.

Potential future features: side-by-side interpretations at different slider positions, "make it weirder," "more era-authentic," regenerate just a name or venue, and editable SVG mockups. Fictional names and logos should be checked before commercial use to avoid confusingly similar real-world identities. No external AI calls or paid services should be enabled by default.
